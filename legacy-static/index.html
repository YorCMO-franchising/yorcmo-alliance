The path from income to equity

Earn. Scale. Own.

We coach Operating CMOs through the FROST framework, leveraging CORE and other mechanisms.

Step 1

Earn

Build a repeatable pipeline and stabilize your income through Cobbler, referrals, collaboration, and work with the flagship firm.

FROST: Focus and Own the Room — a specialization, and the rooms and referral partners that feed it.
Step 2

Scale

Document your methods, productize your offers, and delegate delivery to trusted capacity.

FROST: Resources and System — the people, processes, and technology that make your outcomes repeatable.
Step 3

Own

Develop proprietary IP, recurring offers, an independent client base, and a delivery system that runs without you.

FROST: Trust Transfer — removing the dependency on you, so the practice builds equity over time.
