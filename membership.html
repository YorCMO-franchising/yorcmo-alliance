Questions

Frequently asked

Who is the Alliance for?

Marketing entrepreneurs: fractional and operating CMOs, marketing specialists, consultants and advisors, and marketers building a practice of their own. Members are building a firm they own, not just a calendar of hours.

Do I keep my own brand and clients?

Yes. You build under your own name. Your firm, your self-sourced clients and your intellectual property stay yours. The membership is one flat monthly fee, with no franchise, no royalties and no cut of your client work.

Who is behind it?

The Alliance was founded by Joe Frost, who founded yorCMO in 2016 and the Fractional Professionals Association, created the FROST framework and wrote Million Dollar Fractional Firm. FROST is the path members follow, supported by office hours, roundtables and the member community.

How does joining work?

Membership is by application. You apply, share work samples and two references, and pay a $350 application fee. We review your work, interview you and speak with your references, and you hear from us within 10 business days.

What happens to the application fee?

If you're accepted, it covers your first month. Membership then renews at $350 a month on the same card, and you can manage or cancel it from your account. If you're not accepted, we refund the full $350.

What happens once I'm accepted?

Your account is ready right away. Sign in with your email (we send a one-time link, no password), complete your profile, and the member area, directory and marketplace open up.

How does the marketplace work?

Members list services, delivery capacity, playbooks and products for other members. Each listing is reviewed before it goes live. Buyers contact sellers through the marketplace and agree terms directly.
