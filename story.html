Our story

We spent ten years helping CMOs go independent. Now we help every marketing entrepreneur own what they build.

2016

The bet

Growing companies need senior marketing leadership long before they can justify a full-time CMO. And many of the best CMOs don't want to give one company all of their time.

Joe Frost founded yorCMO to connect the two: experienced CMOs who join a leadership team for a fraction of the time, and stay accountable for the results every week.

To make the work repeatable, we built a shared system, the 6 Fundamentals of Marketing, so every CMO ran the same proven playbook, and every owner knew what they were getting.

The first decade

From going fractional to building practices

At first, the job was helping great CMOs become fractional. Then it became helping fractional CMOs build successful practices. More than 25 CMOs have run the 6 Fundamentals for more than 100 companies.

Joe saw that fractional leaders had nowhere to find each other, so he founded the Fractional Professionals Association, a community for fractional executives across marketing, finance, sales, operations and technology.

What we learned

Two problems that wouldn't go away

Pipeline. The work was excellent; finding the next client wasn't. As one CMO put it to us: "I don't have a pipeline. That's the biggest issue." Many had done unpaid work for referral partners and still never ended up in front of buyers.

Value that walks out the door. Every company that hires an independent marketer counts on their expertise to build value in its business. When they stop showing up, the pay stops, and the systems, the team and the know-how stay behind. They build equity for everyone but themselves.

Owners have the mirror image of the problem. When the person running marketing leaves, marketing starts over. We call it the marketing reset.

Today

The Alliance

Those problems were never just a CMO problem. The SEO specialist, the brand consultant, the content strategist and the marketer going out on their own all live them too. And they need each other: leaders need trusted hands to deliver, and specialists need the work leaders bring in.

So we're spending the next decade on a different goal: helping marketing entrepreneurs build firms with their own clients, their own intellectual property, and delivery that doesn't depend on their calendar. The Alliance is the community where they do it together.

The Alliance puts leads first, with a book, speaking and a community that put members in rooms owners already trust. It gives members leverage through Cobbler and the FROST framework, and a marketplace to sell what they know to each other.

Why Joe built FROST

Most fractionals accidentally rebuild the job they escaped.

Running a fractional CMO firm, Joe watched talented executives leave corporate for freedom, then become the chief problem-solver, the inbox hostage, the scope-creep sponge. Not because they lacked skill, but because no one gave them a system. He traced it to five distractions.

  1. Chasing leads instead of building lead sources. Pros don't chase leads. Pros build faucets.
  2. Fighting scope creep instead of monetizing it. When extra work appears, bring in a partner and profit.
  3. Selling agnostic instead of specializing. Generalists grind. Specialists scale.
  4. Maximizing income instead of optimizing ROI. The goal is not to work more. It's to earn smarter.
  5. Trading hours instead of building equity. Most fractionals sell the one thing they cannot scale: their own time.
The path members follow

The FROST framework

FROST is Joe's strategy system for fractional executives who are ready to stop being contractors and start being firm owners. In the Alliance, members work through it in three stages: Earn, Scale, Own.

F
Earn

Focus

The first domino

Specialize, sharpen your message, and choose the niche you can dominate. Your niche isn't a limitation; it's the lever that makes you impossible to ignore.

R
Scale

Resourcing

The engine of scale

Build your bench: team, peers, partners, vendors and lead sources. Do only what only you can do. Everything else is a resourcing decision.

O
Earn

Own Your Market

Authority over attention

Become the category-of-one in your niche with a signature framework, offer and content. You don't win because you're talented. You win because you're known.

S
Scale

Systems

Built to scale without you

Workflows, SOPs, evergreen engines and AI workers, so you shift from operator to owner. Your future firm is human, outsourced and AI.

T
Own

Transfer Trust

From person to platform

Move trust from you, to your systems, to your team, to your firm, until the business is an asset you could sell.

FROST is the blueprint. The Alliance is where you build it. Members get the community, the tools and the peers to put FROST to work in their own firm. Want to go deeper on your own? Joe's book, course, workshop and 1:1 coaching are at frost-framework.com. More about Joe at josephmarkfrost.com.
