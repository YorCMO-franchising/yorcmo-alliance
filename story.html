Our story

We spent ten years helping CMOs go fractional. Now we help them own what they build.

2016

The bet

Growing companies need senior marketing leadership long before they can justify a full-time CMO. And many of the best CMOs don't want to give one company all of their time.

Joe Frost co-founded yorCMO to connect the two: experienced CMOs who join a leadership team for a fraction of the time, and stay accountable for the results every week.

To make the work repeatable, we built a shared system, the 6 Fundamentals of Marketing, so every CMO ran the same proven playbook, and every owner knew what they were getting.

The first decade

From going fractional to building practices

At first, the job was helping great CMOs become fractional. Then it became helping fractional CMOs build successful practices. More than 25 CMOs have run the 6 Fundamentals for more than 100 companies.

Joe saw that fractional leaders had nowhere to find each other, so he founded the Fractional Professionals Association, a community for fractional executives across marketing, finance, sales, operations and technology.

And the support we gave our CMOs grew up too. It started as a concierge service: a coordinator, a shared plan, and a weekly rhythm. It became CORE, a marketing operating system that runs a client's marketing week after week.

What we learned

Two problems that wouldn't go away

Pipeline. The work was excellent; finding the next client wasn't. As one CMO put it to us: "I don't have a pipeline. That's the biggest issue." Many had done unpaid work for referral partners and still never ended up in front of buyers.

Value that walks out the door. Every company that hires a CMO counts on their expertise to build value in its business. When the CMO stops showing up, the pay stops, and the systems, the team and the know-how stay behind. The CMO builds equity for everyone but themselves.

Owners have the mirror image of the problem. When the person running marketing leaves, marketing starts over. We call it the marketing reset.

Today

The Alliance

So we're spending the next decade on a different goal: helping fractional executives become equity executives, leaders who build firms with their own clients, their own intellectual property, and delivery that doesn't depend on their calendar.

The Alliance puts leads first, with a book, speaking and a community that put members in rooms owners already trust. It gives members leverage through Cobbler and CORE, and a marketplace to sell what they know to each other.

Founder and coach

Meet Joe Frost

Joe has spent 25 years building companies where trusted specialists come together to do great work, then go on to the next thing.

He built HomeSmartz, a mortgage brokerage, and Frost Media Group, where two decades of film crews showed him how a team of specialists assembled for one project can outperform a permanent staff. Then came yorCMO, the Fractional Professionals Association, and Guided Outsourcing, which builds skilled global teams.

He is a university professor of entrepreneurship, the author of New Media Habits, host of The Fractional C-Suite Retreat podcast and the weekly Cup of Joe LinkedIn Live, and a keynote speaker on the future of work on three continents. He lives in Omaha, Nebraska.

"What you know should build something you own, in your own firm."
How Joe coaches

The FROST framework

FROST is Joe's system for turning a fractional practice into a firm that builds equity. Alliance members are coached through it in three stages.

Earn

Focus and Own the Room

Pick a specialization, and find the rooms and referral partners that feed it. Goal: a repeatable pipeline and steady income.

Scale

Resources and System

The people, processes and technology that make your outcomes repeatable. Goal: delivery that doesn't need you in every meeting.

Own

Trust Transfer

Remove the dependency on you, so clients trust the firm, not just the founder. Goal: a practice that builds equity over time.
